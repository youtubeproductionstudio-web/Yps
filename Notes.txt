A Big Stability Update Has Arrived!

We're excited to bring you a major stability update focused on improving accessibility, gameplay reliability, and overall performance.

What's New in This Update?

Fixed the gesture bug. The swipe up with two fingers, then left gesture now correctly opens the Inventory.

Fixed the reload gesture. The swipe down with two fingers, then left gesture now works properly and consistently for reloading.

Added improved Inventory behavior. When the Inventory is opened, the game automatically pauses, preventing any gameplay actions from continuing in the background. Once the Inventory is closed, the game resumes exactly where you left off.

Resolved several on-click issues that could cause buttons or actions to behave unexpectedly.

Fixed multiple runtime errors to improve game stability and reduce crashes.

Improved overall responsiveness and reliability for a smoother gaming experience.

Thank you for playing, and enjoy the update!
