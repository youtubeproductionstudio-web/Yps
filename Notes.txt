Major Stability Update Before Online Multiplayer

We are excited to share that a major stability update is being completed before the launch of Online Multiplayer. All of our servers are now online and running on a new infrastructure to provide a faster, more reliable, and smoother gaming experience.

As part of this upgrade, we migrated our services to a new server platform. Unfortunately, this migration resulted in the loss of previous player data, and some old progress, items, and records may no longer be available.

We sincerely apologize for this inconvenience and appreciate your understanding and support during this transition. To thank you for your patience and help with the recovery process, every affected player will receive a special compensation gift. This reward will be distributed alongside the upcoming major Online Multiplayer update.

Thank you for being part of our community. Your support helps us continue improving the game and delivering exciting new features.
