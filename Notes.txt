The ad reward system has been improved. Players will still normally receive 2 coins for watching an advertisement, but the chances of receiving a bonus reward of 3 coins have been increased, giving players more opportunities to earn extra coins while enjoying the game.

The game has also been further optimized and made more stable to provide smoother performance, better reliability, and an improved overall user experience.

Additionally, in the Memory game mode, a small delay/spacing has been added between the computer opening the first box and the second box, making the gameplay easier to follow and improving the overall visual and gameplay experience.
