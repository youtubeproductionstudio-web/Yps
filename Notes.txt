Major Update Released!

We are excited to announce a major update packed with new features, improvements, and stability enhancements.

What's New?

A new Text-to-Speech (TTS) Settings option has been added. Players can now choose their preferred TTS voice and enjoy a more personalized experience.

Overall game performance has been significantly improved for smoother gameplay.

Numerous bugs, crashes, and stability issues have been fixed.

Gameplay balancing and optimization have been completed to provide a more consistent and enjoyable experience for all players.

The Sunday Gift system provided by the developers now includes stronger validation and security checks to ensure fair distribution and prevent issues.


Upcoming Sound Update

The previously available sounds remain fully functional and stable. However, the new Free Fire-inspired sound effects included in the latest development work are not being released in this update. They are scheduled to arrive in a separate sound update, expected either later this evening or tomorrow morning.

Because this update already contains many major changes, we have decided to release the new sounds separately. This helps reduce the risk of unexpected errors and ensures a more stable experience for everyone.

Need Help?

If you encounter any bugs, errors, or unexpected behavior, please let us know. Open the About section in the game and tap the Send Feedback button to report the issue. Our team will carefully review your report and make every effort to fix the problem as quickly as possible.

Thank you for playing and supporting the game. Your feedback helps us continue improving the experience for everyone. Stay tuned for the upcoming sound update! 🎮🚀
